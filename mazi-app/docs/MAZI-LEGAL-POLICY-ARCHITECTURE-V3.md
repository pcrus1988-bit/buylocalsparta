## EU/Greek statute-to-policy allocation
- GDPR Regulation (EU) 2016/679 arts 5, 6, 12–22, 28–30, 32–34, 44–49; Law 4624/2019 → Privacy, Retention, Cookies.
- Greek Law 3471/2006 art 4(5) and GDPR consent → Cookies; marketing contact permissions → Privacy and Marketing.
- DSA Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 arts 14, 16–17 (where hosting duties apply) → General Terms and Community. The application of additional *online platform* duties must be assessed against restricted registration and the actual audience.
- Regulation (EU) 2019/1150 P2B → Case-specific review of qualifying business-to-consumer intermediation; **not presumed** for a manually admitted, purely B2B member directory.
- Greek Civil Code arts 173, 200, 281, 288, 330, 332, 409 → General Terms, Membership, Award, B2B Marketplace.
- Greek Law 4308/2014 art 7 → Retention and Compliance, only for actual accounting records; separate privacy retention for other data.
- Greek Law 5144/2024 art 16 → service-token VAT classification review. No unsupported assertion that internal noncash tokens are automatically SPV, MPV or electronic money.
- Art 101 TFEU and Greek Law 3959/2011 → Community and Governance; no price fixing or sensitive competitor information sharing.
- Regulation (EU) 2023/988, as actually applicable to product categories/platform function → B2B terms, Community.
- Regulation (EU) 2023/2831 de minimis only if public *state aid* criteria are satisfied → Award, no assumption for purely private resources.

## Enforceability and deployment gates
1. Verify the updated MAZI pages and all cross-references with production browser checks.
2. Verify versioned acceptance for *paid* terms before activating billing; the existing invitation consent cannot be reused to charge a member.
3. Verify Greek lawyer review of long mandatory membership retention and proportionate termination. Exact terms in an individualized award must match what is signed.
4. Verify accountant/Greek VAT review of membership, service entitlements, credits and private business grants, with lawful invoicing.
5. Test Fund financial ledger/role separation, exact server-side cycle schedule, vote uniqueness and payout gates; no actual disbursement until tests and legal reviews pass.
6. Complete actual personal-data inventory, GDPR records of processing, processors and transfer mechanisms, cookies audit/consent and tested data-retention executions. The policy does not imply that unverified deletion automation already exists.
7. Archive accepted legal text version/hash and timestamps. Publish material changes without representing website publication as retroactive individual consent.
8. Never infer financial receipts, fulfilled ad campaigns, transferred membership funds or completed grants merely from the existence of a policy.
