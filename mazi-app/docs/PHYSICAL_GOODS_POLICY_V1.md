# MAZI physical goods marketplace — mazi-goods-v1
Effective: 2026-10-27. This version ID is persisted with each new or edited listing and timestamp.
Audience: approved, GEMI-verified member businesses only.

1. Allowed listings: tangible, lawful physical goods; offers or requests. Accurate owner rights, condition, quantity, city, delivery terms, category, and description are mandatory.
2. Prohibited: nonphysical services, jobs, loans, financial products; stolen, fake, infringing or prohibited goods; controlled or hazardous products without mandatory lawful authorization; weapons, explosives, recreational drugs, tobacco/nicotine, prescription medicines, and content containing third-party personal data.
3. MAZI is a member discovery and private communication service only. No purchase contract, custody, checkout, payment, invoice, shipping, tax calculation or guarantees are provided by MAZI.
4. Prices are indicative and VAT basis is disclosed as included, excluded or unresolved. The parties must agree written commercial terms and comply with all applicable Greek/EU fiscal, product-safety, consumer and B2B rules.
5. Listings expire after 60 days. Owners may modify active listings, renew within the final 14 days, or close them. Moderation reports are available. Hidden listings must not generate new inquiries; preexisting private inquiry history remains governed by the separate privacy/retention terms.
6. Users must not expose third-party private data, home addresses, phone numbers or AFM in listings. Private messaging is member-restricted and subject to moderation and applicable retention.
7. The policy checkbox is mandatory on create and every edit, with policy version and consent timestamp recorded. New policy versions require a new acceptance.
8. The service may suspend dangerous, misleading or unauthorized posts; legal review of marketplace-specific B2B responsibilities and sector restrictions remains necessary before accepting materially different regulated product classes.
