# DrinkStock operational domain — October 2026
This is the first persistent data-model foundation. It **does not enable charging, guest order submission, kitchen dispatch or receipt issuance**.

## Key invariants
- Multi-tenancy: every venue belongs to exactly one partner, using composite venue/partner FK. All server-side reads require verified membership, except narrowly scoped opaque public screen/table passes.
- Sessions are unique per venue (only one non-ended session). A session snapshots pricing bounds and has scheduled → running ↔ paused; running → crashing for 60 seconds → running; any active state → ended. Ended sessions cannot reopen.
- A token grants only its specific screen or table visibility, never manager access. Store SHA-256 hashes, not raw token values. Rotate/revoke per venue. Users from other locations can open HTTPS public screen URLs, but the screen does not expose manager controls or customer data.
- A printed table QR is bound to a single venue/table and requires a running session for orders; QR reuse does not grant access to other venues. Expire or revoke compromised tokens.
- Monetary prices are immutable integer-cent snapshots at order acceptance, not subsequently recalculated when the trading board moves. A quote has a 15-second acceptance window; expiration requires customer reconfirmation.
- Fulfilment: awaiting_payment → paid → accepted → preparing → ready → served. Never dispense based on unverified browser payment state. Payment provider webhooks are verified and idempotent.
- Fiscal: the registered hospitality merchant is seller of record. Classify VAT per item and service type, with venue-specific statutory tax rules reviewed by an accountant. Receipts must come from a legally authorized Greek retail fiscal device/provider; a payment success notification is not a fiscal receipt. Issue/credit with unique external references and reconcile failures.
- Refunds must not erase the original receipt. Corresponding credit documents and payment refunds are tracked separately.
- The demo trading feed must never be used as an input to billing or accounting.
- All writes require venue scoping, idempotency keys, authorization, row locks, and auditable state transitions.

## Implementation status (October 19, 2026)
- Manager/owner session start, pause, resume, crash for 60 seconds, end and access-pass revocation are implemented on the feature branch.
- Pre-session venue price boundaries are configurable from 50–100% (minimum) and 100–200% (maximum), initially 70–145%. These settings and catalogue additions are locked by the server for the full lifetime of any active operational session, including pause/crash, and while rehearsal is running. Session startup snapshots the configured limits.
- Remote view-only screens show the venue board, visible crash countdown, red warning corners, opt-in/mutable alarm, and a feed of **manual rehearsal demand signals**, never claims of actual paid orders. Disconnected or revoked screens hide cached prices.
- Venue staff have an authenticated fulfilment queue that accepts only payment-provider-verified paid orders. The legal receipt gate prevents marking orders served unless fiscal issuance was confirmed. Since providers are not connected, the queue will remain empty by design.
- Printed per-table QR passes identify a venue and table but remain **read-only**: no guest order submission, payment collection or fiscal receipt creation is activated.
- The QR guest UI now supports a browser-ephemeral cart (maximum 12 drinks/24 total units, 10 of any one item), market/crash awareness and a **15-second price preview**. POST `/api/hospitality/public/table/[token]/quote` resolves the opaque venue/table pass, checks the trading state and validates product IDs/quantities, then recomputes all euro-cent totals from the venue catalogue. The browser cannot dictate prices, partner/venue identity or payment state. Previews carry `checkoutEnabled: false` and never insert orders.
- Previews are deliberately non-binding: product prices may move, and a future commercial checkout must atomically revalidate the session, token, product stock/eligibility, quote freshness and locked product cents before taking payment. Customer-selected previews must never be interpreted as evidence of paid demand.
- Public consumer demo remains isolated from venue and financial records, including during its simulated crash order rush.
- Production migration 0451 was applied and its checksum verified on October 19; operational tables have RLS and no direct `anon`/`authenticated` table grants.
- Operational start refuses while manual rehearsal is enabled. The venue manager must pause rehearsal first. Each new session resets all active drink prices atomically to their base prices, avoiding leftovers from previous sessions or rehearsals. While an operational session is active, manual rehearsal demand, price resets and rehearsal start/pause are denied (even during a crash or operational pause); owner/manager bounds and catalogue changes remain locked.
- Remote screens now display a `PARTY TICKER · DEMO` synthesized from the **same session, server-aligned time and catalogue**. Independent screens at different locations render the same mock purchases every 4.5 seconds, accelerated to every 1.7 seconds throughout the 60-second crash, with real-time product highlighting, emergency corners and opt-in alarm. This presentation feed is strictly non-commercial: no writes to orders, payment/fiscal ledgers or actual drink prices; no guessed customer order events. During operations the rehearsal-history feed is hidden; historical rehearsal signals are filtered from the active session's start.
- Owner/manager can print one-time venue/table or remote-TV QR codes and dismiss the unpersisted full token. Rotation/revocation still happens on the server.

## Activation gates
1. Apply version 0451 to production through the existing versioned schema deployment process; keep the schema ledger and SHA checksums in sync.
2. Implement authenticated manager controls, QR/screen token lifecycle, public scoped readers, and transactionally locked session actions.
3. Choose and configure a regulated PSP with signature-verified webhooks, secure tokenized payment collection, refunds, and merchant settlement.
4. Connect the approved Greek fiscal receipt mechanism/provider, verify VAT policy and service context, and test receipt/credit reconciliation.
5. Run concurrent cross-tenant, fake-payment, price-change, crash-window, token-revocation, and duplicate-webhook tests before enabling real guest checkout.

## Screen source control (TV feed mode, October 19 2026)
- Each **screen access pass** has its own persistent `feed_mode`, default `simulation`. Existing screen URLs continue to function. A venue owner/manager can toggle a specific screen in their venue dashboard; a platform admin with `vendor.manage` can also change it from `/admin/hospitality`. Both mutate through audited, idempotent, server-side writes, not screen URLs or guest commands.
- **Simulation:** synchronized, clearly tagged fake trades continue moving across displays, including throughout the 60-second crash. No simulated trade is inserted into the order, payment, price or fiscal ledgers.
- **Actual Orders:** queries only DrinkStock orders belonging to the **same partner, venue, and current session**, with `payment_state='paid'`, eligible paid workflow status and a persisted `drinkstock_payment_events.event_type='payment_verified'` event. Product IDs must join products in the same partner/venue. If any requirement fails the item is not shown. All output is limited to drink description, quantity and payment-verification timestamp; no table labels, customer identity, order ID, transaction amount or external reference is exposed. Paid order reversals/cancellations no longer appear after next polling.
- Actual mode **never falls back to artificial buying** when there are zero paid orders. The screen shows a visible empty state. Both modes retain session status, price board, red crash corners, opt-in sound and 60-second countdown; screen access remains revocable.
- The real guest PSP/fiscal integrations are not enabled yet, so production Actual mode normally shows an empty state until a separately reviewed, signature-verified `payment_verified` provider adapter is implemented. Never insert synthetic paid records to populate the feed.
- Migration 0452 adds the feed selector, action audit classification and a venue/session-specific partial index. Only backend DB connections receive access, with RLS and browser-role revokes preserved.

## Verified order evidence and partner reporting (October 19, 2026)
- Staff order queue, fulfilment transitions, TV actual-order stream and financial reports all require an original `drinkstock_payment_events` row with `event_type='payment_verified'`, exact `amount_cents=order.amount_gross_cents`, a non-demo provider, nonblank provider event ID and a 64-character lowercase SHA-256 payload digest. A browser `payment_state='paid'` claim, a rehearsal `demand`, or an unverified event never authorizes serving or appears as commercial trading.
- The provider adapter **does not exist yet**. A future adapter must verify the provider's authenticity server-to-server, exact EUR amount/order scope, deduplicate provider event IDs, write the event and order transition atomically, and explicitly separate sandbox events from production. Setting a DB row's paid flag is insufficient.
- Serving requires both `order.fiscal_state='issued'` and a `drinkstock_fiscal_documents` `retail_receipt` row with state `issued`, issued timestamp and nonempty provider document ID or MARK. Staff cannot issue receipts or bypass this gate. The queue indicates a verified receipt separately.
- The formerly placeholder `/drinkstock/dashboard/[partnerId]/reports` route is now a real **authenticated owner/manager-only** trading intelligence dashboard. Filters: 7/30/90 days. Data: venue totals, session history, per-day paid order value, top purchased drinks, separate rehearsal signal counts, active TV source modes and reversal-affected order counts.
- Monetary numbers represent current recorded gross value of valid provider-verified *paid and not reversed* orders, **not VAT or recognized accounting revenue**. Reversed/partially reversed orders are counted separately; the UI avoids overstating unknown refund amounts. No provisional or simulated figures are inserted and zero commercial activity is shown as zero. All SQL is restricted to the authenticated partner, with product-to-venue joins on reported drinks; staff, public tables and TV screens do not gain access.
- Reports require no schema changes beyond the already-applied 0452. Payment and fiscal provider activation remains explicitly disabled.

## Live TV simulation pricing (October 19, 2026)
- The venue manager's independent **TV screen source selector** remains per pass: `simulation` or `actual`. In **simulation** mode only, each public /live screen computes **single-cent** demo prices from the **same session ID, server-aligned clock, time-weighted history of up to 48 deterministic buy events and session-snapshotted owner bounds**.
- Demo trade cadence remains 4.5 seconds, accelerated to 1.7 seconds during the 60-second crash. **Trading continues during the crash**. Recent buys are weighted by recency and quantity: products outperforming the average buy volume rise; under-bought products ease. No arbitrary sine-wave price changes and no five-cent snapping. Crash pressure is applied at trade ticks toward (never below) the configured floor. Identical inputs on different remote TVs result in identical prices; calculations require no shared client state and run in bounded time.
- Every screen price is labeled **DEMO PRICE**. A distinct strip explains that simulated values are illustrations only, not guest/cart quotes. Relative movement, starting prices and simulated price bands appear near each drink. Real-order mode uses the unchanged persisted venue price, and the table QR cart continues to obtain only **server-authoritative** prices from `drinkstock_products.price_cents`; no UI-only value can be charged or stored.
- This feature **does not** implement the live commercial price engine, POS bridge, order submission, PSP settlement, VAT calculation or fiscal issuance. The simulated price function neither queries nor writes orders, payments, market events, product prices or invoices.

## Cent-level demand engine update (October 19, 2026)
- The server-persisted **manual rehearsal** market also uses integer **€0.01** increments, rather than multiples of €0.05. The venue owner/manager can issue controlled buy signals of 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 units. A stronger signal increases the chosen drink more and decreases its unbought peers; every change is recorded with its unit count in the existing JSON movements audit. No schema change or consumer checkout capability is introduced.
- Both demo and rehearsal price floors use `ceil(base_cents × floor_percent / 100)`; ceilings use `floor(base_cents × ceiling_percent / 100)`, without a five-cent grid. All state/quotes are **integer cents**, never floating euro values. E.g., a €1.01 reference with 70%–145% limits has a €0.71–€1.46 band (prior five-cent limits no longer apply).
- **Existing safety bands still apply.** A €0.32 absolute price is outside current database/product restrictions (base listing ≥€1; live minimum ≥€0.50) and would need a separately approved bounds/catalogue schema change. An arbitrary cents suffix such as €3.32 or €4.87 is supported when inside the venue band.
- The public `/drinkstock` landing-page auto-play now uses the **same unit-sensitive cent-level pricing helper** as the private rehearsal. The predefined order sequence stays unchanged; a 3-drink order is evaluated as one larger-volume trade, not three artificial +7% price jumps. Crash shocks round to the cent. This is still strictly a locally animated, browser-only demonstration.
- Simulation TV prices remain **non-commercial visualizations**. Actual mode and guest quote API keep using **persisted, server-authoritative cents**. Operational order-driven repricing must await a separately verified, tenant-scoped payment-event adapter with atomic market updates before checkout can be enabled.

## Market trajectory on venue boards and remote TV screens (October 20, 2026)
- Each partner-specific **rehearsal trading-floor card** now plots a compact, accessible price trend from its **persisted** venue-scoped `drinkstock_market_events.movements` already returned by the authorized market snapshot. History is capped at the latest 20 events; no direct database read, analytics permission or new column is added. If the persisted current price and event chain disagree, the chart is hidden rather than inventing missing points.
- Each view-only simulation TV produces the latest **up to 12 executed demo-tick prices**, computed from its existing session ID, shared server-aligned clock, venue catalogue and snapshotted bounds. The series advances only on 4.5-second normal trade executions or 1.7-second crash trades, and restarts its chart window when the 60-second crash phase starts. Like the tape itself, this is illustrative and never affects stored prices.
- **Actual paid-order** TV mode deliberately displays neither the demo history nor simulated price movements. The guest quote service continues to calculate prices only from server-persisted integer cents. No customer names, venue-foreign records, unverified payments or table identifiers appear in price charts.
- Chart coordinates are bounded and tested, remain legible without color alone through explicit screen-reader labels, and obey the existing 60-product limit. No new migration or POS/PSP integration is required.
