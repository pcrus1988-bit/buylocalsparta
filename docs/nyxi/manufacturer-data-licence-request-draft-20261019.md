# NYXI — Manufacturer data-licence request packet (draft; not sent)

This is a reusable proposal to send **only after identifying and verifying the correct brand/data-licensing contact**. Do not infer an email address from the site's contact domain and do not email anyone automatically.

**Suggested subject:** Request for authorised nail-product data feed, SDS and shade metadata — NYXI

Hello,

We are building NYXI, a standalone nail-product intelligence and colour discovery service focused on helping people identify nail-product lines, shades, ingredient information, product-safety notices and compatible routines. Our application is designed to work with different authorised retailers and ecommerce shops through an API.

We would like to request your **written permission and preferred technical integration method** to use, archive for verification, and periodically refresh your official public product information for NYXI.

We are interested in a structured manufacturer-authorised dataset for:
- Brand/legal entity, market, product line, polish category and finish.
- Product SKU, EAN/GTIN (where available), original shade name/code, colour-family tags, sizes and localised label versions.
- Colour measurement data if available (sRGB/HEX with measurement method, spectral/Lab data, lighting/illuminant and substrate; whether colour is indicative or calibrated).
- Current and historical INCI ingredient statements, especially **formula revision, market/region, effective date and batch variation**, separately identified from ingredient marketing claims.
- SDS documents and version dates for each applicable product or product family.
- Authoritative product-safety notices, documented reformulations and withdrawals.
- Approved asset download/update mechanism (API, CSV, XML, JSON, versioned S3 link) and recommended refresh rate.

Please tell us which parts you permit NYXI to **display publicly**, which may be **stored privately as immutable provenance** (date/hash/version/source URL), whether product photos or colour swatches may be reused, applicable attribution requirements and any licence fees or limitations. We will not represent approximate colour samples as laboratory measurements, infer a formula for an SKU without evidence, or claim endorsement.

We respect the terms of your website and are not requesting to bypass any technical access restrictions. A manufacturer-approved feed would be preferred over automated collection from the website.

Kind regards,

NYXI Research & Product Integration
research@kontamou.site
https://nyxi.kontamou.site
